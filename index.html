Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Blue Flame warranties, rebates, and emergency dispatch.

How fast can a Blue Flame technician arrive at my home in Joliet?
During standard service calls, same-day appointments are guaranteed when booked before 12:00 PM. In emergency heating freeze warnings, our on-call radio-dispatched trucks stationed across Joliet and Will County average an on-site response time between 45 and 90 minutes.
What rebates are available for high-efficiency heating and cooling in Illinois?
As a registered Nicor Gas and ComEd Energy Efficiency Partner, Blue Flame qualifies homeowners for qualifying utility and manufacturer rebates. We deduct eligible rebates directly from your invoice so you don't wait months for checks.
What warranty protection comes with a new Blue Flame installation?
All qualifying Carrier Factory-Authorized installations include a 10-Year Limited Parts Warranty, Lifetime Heat Exchanger Warranty (on premier furnaces), and Blue Flame's exclusive 1-Year 100% Labor Guarantee with complimentary first-year seasonal maintenance tune-up.
Do you offer financing options for complete furnace or AC replacements?
Yes! We provide flexible 0% APR financing for up to 60 months with approved credit, as well as low-monthly payment plans with no prepayment penalties. Approval takes less than 5 minutes online or on-site with your technician.
