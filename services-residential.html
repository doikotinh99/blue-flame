Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Residential HVAC Questions

When should I repair vs replace an aging furnace or AC? −
As a standard rule of thumb (the $5,000 Rule): Multiply the age of your equipment by the repair cost estimate. If the total exceeds $5,000, or if your furnace is over 15 years old with cracked heat exchangers, replacing it with a 96%+ AFUE system is substantially more cost-effective due to utility savings and avoiding cascading part failures.
Can cold-climate heat pumps really heat during sub-zero Chicago freezes? +
Yes! Modern inverter heat pumps with hyper-heating vapor injection produce 100% rated heat output down to -5°F and operate efficiently down to -13°F. In our dual-fuel configurations, the heat pump handles mild winter days (30°F - 55°F) at 300% thermodynamic efficiency, and switches seamlessly to the high-efficiency gas furnace when arctic vortexes strike.
How do the ComEd and Nicor Gas rebates work? +
As certified ComEd Energy Efficiency Service Providers and Nicor Gas Trade Allies, Blue Flame applies your rebates directly as an instant discount on your invoice. You don't have to wait months for mail-in rebate checks—we handle all program filings, AHRI certificates, and compliance paperwork for you.
What is included in your 26-point home tune-up? +
Our comprehensive tune-up includes: testing carbon monoxide levels and combustion safety, checking heat exchanger integrity with digital camera scopes, cleaning flame sensors and burners, measuring motor amp draw and capacitor microfarads, chemical condenser coil cleaning, testing static duct pressures, calibrating thermostats, and verifying subcooling/superheat refrigerant levels.
