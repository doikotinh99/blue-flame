Plant Operations

Overcoming Complex Industrial Plant Obstacles

How our mechanical engineers safeguard mission-critical processes from failure.

Sub-Zero Winter Cooling Tower Freezing

When winter wind chills hit -15°F in the Chicago area, open cooling tower basins can freeze into solid ice blocks within hours. We design automated dry-sump indoor drain-down systems, dual-speed fan de-icing cycles, and modulating 3-way bypass valves that protect towers year-round.

High Boiler Fuel Costs & Stack Losses

Excessive flue gas temperatures waste hundreds of thousands of dollars in natural gas annually. We integrate condensing stack economizers that capture heat from the chimney flue to preheat boiler makeup feedwater, slashing fuel consumption by 6% to 12%.

Unplanned Chiller Trips During Peak Production

When high condensing head pressures trigger safety lockouts in 95°F July heat, manufacturing assembly lines halt instantly. We engineer N+1 redundant parallel chiller circuits with automatic failover, eliminating single points of catastrophic failure.

Industrial FAQs

Frequently Asked Plant Mechanical Questions

What are the benefits of oil-free magnetic-bearing chillers? −
Traditional centrifugal chillers suffer up to a 10-15% efficiency penalty over time due to lubricating oil coating the internal copper heat exchanger tubes. Magnetic-bearing chillers (such as Danfoss Turbocor) use permanent magnetic levitation to suspend the shaft in air without mechanical contact. They require zero oil, produce virtually zero vibration, boast industry-leading IPLV ratings as low as 0.31 kW/Ton, and have negligible starting inrush current.
How do you keep cooling towers from freezing during Illinois winter operation? +
We employ three key winterization methods: 1) Indoor remote sump basins located inside the heated mechanical room, allowing water to drain out of the outdoor tower instantly upon shutdown; 2) Electric immersion basin heaters with low-water cutoffs; and 3) Modulating 3-way diversion valves paired with VFD fan reversal controls to periodically de-ice air intake louvers.
What does eddy current non-destructive tube testing reveal? +
Eddy current testing uses electromagnetic induction probes passed through chiller evaporator and condenser copper tubes to detect microscopic pitting, wall thinning, stress corrosion cracking, and baffle damage before tubes rupture. A tube rupture can flood the entire refrigerant system with water, resulting in catastrophic compressor motor burnout.
Can your mechanical systems interface directly with our factory SCADA/DCS? +
Yes. All our industrial equipment controllers natively communicate via BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP/RTU, and Ethernet/IP protocols. We integrate chiller status, flow rates, boiler flame signals, and temperature loops directly into your Wonderware, Rockwell FactoryTalk, or custom plant SCADA dashboards.
