Critical Commercial Challenges We Overcome

Commercial facilities require specialized planning, code compliance, and structural coordination.

Zero-Interruption Weekend Crane Swaps

We secure FAA crane permits, coordinate police street closures, fabricate custom curb adaptors in advance, and lift units on Saturday morning so your tenants open on Monday with perfect air conditioning.

Restaurant Negative Pressure & Odors

Heavy exhaust hoods pulling 6,000 CFM without equal makeup air suck kitchen smoke and grease into guest seating. Our direct-fired tempered supply systems balance pressure and satisfy local health inspectors.

High Utility Peak Demand Penalties

When dozens of rooftop units kick on simultaneously at 8 AM, commercial electric rates spike violently. We implement staged startup logic, free-cooling economizers, and ComEd rebate-eligible VFD retrofits.

Commercial FAQs

Frequently Asked Commercial HVAC Questions

Can rooftop RTUs be replaced without interrupting customer business hours? −
Yes. We regularly execute commercial crane replacements during off-hours, early Sunday mornings, or overnight. Our team takes care of police traffic details, street blocking permits, and pre-measures the existing roof curb so custom transition adaptors are ready on site. Old units come down and new units are connected and tested before your doors open on Monday morning.
What ComEd energy efficiency rebates are available for commercial businesses? +
Through the ComEd Energy Efficiency Program, commercial customers qualify for substantial instant rebates on high-efficiency RTUs (up to $100 per ton), variable frequency drives (VFDs), smart thermostats, and advanced rooftop unit control retrofits. As an approved Trade Ally, Blue Flame deducts rebates directly from your contract balance.
What does your Commercial Preventative Maintenance Agreement (PMA) include? +
Our standard commercial PMA includes quarterly inspections: belt inspections and tensioning, filter replacements, chemical cleaning of evaporator and condenser coils, electrical connection tightening, bearing lubrication, economizer calibration, heat exchanger safety scope testing, and compliance documentation for local fire and health inspectors.
Do you offer commercial equipment leasing or capital expenditure financing? +
Yes, we partner with leading commercial equipment lenders to offer flexible 24 to 72-month financing and operating leases. In most cases, the monthly utility energy savings from modern high-efficiency RTUs offset the majority of the monthly lease payment, preserving your working capital.
