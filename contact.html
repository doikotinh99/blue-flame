Emergency Guidelines

What to Do While Waiting for Your Technician

Crucial safety steps to safeguard your plumbing and property until our truck pulls into your driveway.

Open under-sink cabinet doors in your kitchen and bathrooms to let warm room air circulate around water supply lines. Turn faucets to a slight, steady trickle—moving water takes far longer to freeze, preventing catastrophic pipe ruptures.
If you detect a distinct rotten egg odor, do NOT flick light switches, turn on appliances, or use your phone inside the home. Immediately evacuate all household members and pets outside, then call Nicor Gas (888-642-6748) or 911 from a safe distance before calling our dispatch desk.
Nearly 15% of winter no-heat calls are caused by dead AA batteries inside older wall thermostats, or someone accidentally flipping the red emergency furnace power toggle switch located at the top of the basement stairs. Check both switches first!
