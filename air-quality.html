Whole-Home Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) Solutions

The EPA ranks indoor air pollution among the top environmental health risks. In Chicago, sealed winter homes trap viruses, wildfire smoke, pet dander, and dry air. We restore pure hospital-grade air to every room.

Complete Defense

The Four Pillars of Chicagoland Clean Air

Standard 1-inch furnace filters only protect the blower motor. Our whole-home solutions sanitize every cubic foot of air your family breathes.

Viruses • Mold • Dust

MERV 16 Medical Filtration

Captures over 99% of airborne particles down to 0.3 microns, including COVID-19, influenza, wildfire smoke particles, and pollen.

  • Up to 500x more effective than basic filters
  • Annual cartridge change (lasts 12 months)
  • Low static resistance on blower motor
Bacteria • VOCs • Odors

In-Duct UV-C Germicidal Lamps

Direct hospital-grade UV-C irradiation destroys the DNA of bacteria, viruses, and microbial slime growing on your central AC evaporator coil.

  • Neutralizes biological pathogens instantly
  • Keeps AC coil pristine and efficient
  • Zero ozone emission certified
Dry Sinuses • Hardwood Cracking

Whole-Home Steam Humidification

Winter heating drops indoor humidity to Sahara Desert levels (<15%). Our automated steam humidifiers maintain the healthy 35-45% sweet spot.

  • Protects hardwood floors & acoustic instruments
  • Relieves nosebleeds, itchy skin & dry throat
  • Makes 68°F feel like 72°F to cut heating bills
Stale Air • Carbon Dioxide • Radon

Energy Recovery Ventilators (ERV)

Continuously exhausts stale, CO2-laden indoor air while drawing in filtered fresh outdoor air, transferring thermal energy to minimize heating loss.

  • Recovers up to 80% of conditioned temperature
  • Reduces heavy basement radon & cooking fumes
  • Essential for modern tight energy-code homes
