Pastoral Engineering

Solving Unique Worship Space Challenges

How Blue Flame engineering eliminates the common headaches of sacred architecture.

Roaring Mechanical Noise During Sermons

Old metal duct systems and noisy blowers rattle during moments of prayer and silence. We install double-wall acoustic silencer baffles, line supply plenums with sound-absorptive media, and size diffusers for ultra-low air discharge velocity.

Massive Inefficient Heating of Empty Space

Heating a 50,000 cu. ft. sanctuary 24/7 when it is only occupied for 6 hours a week bleeds parish financial reserves. We install smart occupancy setbacks that warm the pews 2 hours before service begins and cycle down immediately after.

Chilly Pews Under 40-Foot Timber Vaults

Heat natural convection leaves the ceiling at 80°F while the congregation in the pews sits at 62°F. Our silent, low-RPM architectural destratification fans equalize room temperatures from top to bottom, cutting heating costs by up to 30%.

Parish FAQs

Frequently Asked Church HVAC Questions

How do you guarantee the heating/cooling system won't make noise during prayer? −
We design church systems around Noise Criterion (NC) ratings rather than standard commercial guidelines. By oversized low-velocity ductwork, incorporating acoustic sound-absorbing elbow baffles, using variable-speed ECM blower motors that ramp up smoothly, and installing mechanical equipment either in remote basements or on exterior vibration isolation springs, we ensure equipment operation stays below NC-25—inaudible from the pews.
Can we heat just the parish offices without heating the massive sanctuary? +
Yes, absolutely. That is one of the biggest energy savers we implement. By installing separate multi-zone ductless mini-split heat pumps for the administrative offices, rectory, and nursery, your parish can keep staff perfectly comfortable Monday through Friday while the massive main sanctuary rests in an energy-saving setback mode (such as 55°F).
Do churches and religious non-profits qualify for ComEd and Nicor Gas rebates? +
Yes! Churches, synagogues, mosques, and religious non-profit facilities qualify for both standard and custom incentives through the ComEd Energy Efficiency Program and the Nicor Gas Energy Efficiency Program. Rebates are available for high-efficiency heat pumps, condensing boilers, smart worship scheduling thermostats, and destratification fans. We handle 100% of the grant paperwork.
Can you present your proposal directly to our parish council or finance board? +
Yes, our leadership team regularly attends evening church council, board of trustees, and diocesan finance meetings to present clear, non-technical slideshows detailing the scope of work, return on investment, rebate credits, and phased capital expenditure schedules to answer all committee questions directly.
